    WHO WE ARE

    Built by the community. Driven by ambition.

    Timau Union FC is a community-owned football club based in Timau, Meru County, Kenya. Players, supporters, local businesses and well-wishers share a commitment to growing football in our community.

    The club finished second in its zone and qualified for the playoffs in both the 2025 and 2026 editions of the Meru County League. We aim to build on that progress through better organisation, consistent preparation and community support.

OUR CLUB

Built by the community

Timau Union FC is a community-owned football club based in Timau, Meru County. The club brings players, supporters, local businesses and well-wishers together around a shared love of football.

The team finished second in its zone and qualified for the playoffs in both the 2025 and 2026 editions of the Meru County League. We continue to work towards a stronger and more sustainable future for the club.

OUR STORY

More than a football club

Timau Union FC is rooted in the community. The club's progress depends on committed players, responsible leadership, supporters and partners working together.

Our ambition is to build a competitive, organised and sustainable club while creating opportunities for local football talent.

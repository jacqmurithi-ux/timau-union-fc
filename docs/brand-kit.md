# Timau Union FC — Sponsor Brand Kit

Use these assets and rules when preparing sponsor-facing material.

## Official crest
- [Open the official club crest](../assets/club-crest.jpg)
- Use the supplied image without recolouring, cropping away key elements, stretching, or adding effects.
- Keep clear space around the crest and preserve its original proportions.
- Do not place the crest over a busy background that reduces legibility.

## Club colours
- **Club red:** `#c8102e`
- **White:** `#ffffff`
- **Dark text:** `#222222`

Use club red for primary headings and accents, white for clean backgrounds, and dark text for readable body copy.

## Typography and layout
- Use the same clean sans-serif style as the club website.
- Keep layouts mobile-friendly, with readable contrast and short copy.
- Use the full club name, **TIMAU UNION FC**, on first reference.
- Do not imply a sponsor partnership or benefit that has not been approved by the club.

## Sponsor approvals
- Logos, sponsor descriptions, partnership announcements and benefit claims must be approved by the Sponsor Lead before public release.
- Only use media the Media Team has explicitly marked as shareable for the relevant sponsor.

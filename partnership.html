Ways to partner

Find the right fit

Choose the area that best matches your business or organisation. Benefits, duration, price and delivery dates are agreed with the club before a package is confirmed. Examples below are options, not automatic guarantees.

Kit

Help fund playing or training kit so the team can represent Timau with pride.

  • Agreed logo or business-name placement on selected kit, subject to club approval
  • Recognition in club updates and a team-kit thank-you
  • Team photos for agreed sponsor use
Enquire about this package

Match day

Help make a fixture or football event possible while introducing your business to local supporters.

  • Sponsor acknowledgement in agreed pre-match and post-match posts
  • Recognition on match posters and at the event where arrangements allow
  • Optional banner, product display or photo opportunity by agreement
Enquire about this package

Training

Support training equipment, sessions or player preparation throughout the season.

  • Recognition as a training supporter on club channels
  • Agreed name or logo placement on training bibs or equipment, where available
  • Thank-you post and an agreed training-session photo
Enquire about this package

Website / social

Reach the club community through digital channels and approved sponsor content.

  • Business name, logo and website link on the partners page, once approved
  • Agreed sponsor feature or thank-you posts
  • Logo or name on selected match graphics where suitable
Enquire about this package

Community partner

Back community football and help the club create positive opportunities around Timau.

  • Recognition as a community partner on the website, once approved
  • Agreed thank-you posts and mentions in club announcements
  • Opportunity to discuss a community activity with the club
Enquire about this package

Other

Propose cash support, equipment, services, transport, refreshments or another practical contribution.

  • Benefits tailored to the agreed support
  • Suitable recognition discussed before anything is promised
  • Cash and in-kind contributions documented separately in the agreement
